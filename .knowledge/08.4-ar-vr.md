# AR and VR Support

MMGIS supports Augmented Reality (AR) viewing of 3D globe data on supported devices.

## AR Mode

On AR-supported browsers:
1. Open a mission and open the globe panel
2. Click the semi-transparent eye icon in the globe panel to enable AR
3. Allow camera access when prompted
4. Point the camera around to identify surfaces

AR mode overlays the 3D globe data onto the real-world view through the device camera.

## Requirements

- AR-supported browser (WebXR)
- Device with camera access
- Globe panel must be open
